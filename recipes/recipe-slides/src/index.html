Chicken and Couscous

For 2 chicken breasts and 2 cups of couscous.

Chicken Marinade

  • 1 clove grated garlic (optional)
  • 5-10 cracks of salt and pepper each
  • 2 TSP dried oregano flakes
  • 6 TSP lemon juice
  • 4 TBS olive oil

In a bowl, mix all the marinade ingredients.

Couscous

  • 1 TSP salt
  • 4 TBS olive oil
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 cups couscous
  1. Boil the salt, olive oil, and water in a pot.
  2. Once boiled, add the couscous, turn off the heat, and stir.
  3. Place the lid on the pot for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

Couscous Sauce

  • About 4 cracks of salt and pepper each
  • About 4-6 TBS olive oil
  • About 2-4 TBS lemon juice

In a bowl, whisk together the salt, pepper, olive oil, and lemon juice.

Honey Garlic Chicken

Ingredients

  • 500g / 1 lb chicken breast, boneless and skinless (2 pieces)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 1/2 TBS (50g) unsalted butter, or 2 1/2 TBS olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 TBS apple cider vinegar, or white or other clear vinegar
  • 1 TBS soy sauce, light or all purpose
  • 1/3 cup honey, or maple syrup

Instructions

  1. Cut the breasts in half horizontally to create 4 steaks. Sprinkle each side with salt and pepper.
  2. Place flour in a shallow dish. Coat the chicken in flour and shake off the excess.
  3. Melt most of the butter in a large skillet over high heat, holding back about 1 TSP for later.
  4. Place the chicken in the skillet and cook for 2-3 minutes until golden. Turn and cook the other side for 1 minute.
  5. Turn the heat down slightly to medium-high.
  6. Make room in the pan and add the garlic and remaining butter. Stir briefly once the butter melts.
  7. Add the vinegar, soy sauce, and honey. Stir or shake the pan to combine, then simmer for 1 minute or until slightly thickened.
  8. Turn the chicken to coat it in the sauce. If the sauce gets too thick, add a little water and stir.
  9. Remove from the stove immediately. Place the chicken on plates and drizzle over the remaining sauce.

Korean Beef and Rice Bowl

A quick Korean-inspired ground beef and rice bowl made with seasoned beef, steamed rice, and fresh garnishes.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 3 TBS soy sauce
  • 2 TBS brown sugar
  • 1 TBS sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 TSP grated fresh ginger
  • 4 cups cooked jasmine or white rice
  • Sliced green onions and sesame seeds, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and cook until completely browned, breaking it apart as it cooks.
  2. Drain any excess fat.
  3. Stir in the garlic, ginger, soy sauce, brown sugar, and sesame oil.
  4. Simmer for 2-3 minutes, until the sauce slightly thickens and coats the beef.
  5. Serve over cooked rice and garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.

Homemade Pizza

This is a quick and easy recipe for non-rising dough homemade pizza.

Ingredients

  • 3 Cups Flour
  • 1 TSP Salt
  • 1 TSP Sugar
  • 1 TBS Olive Oil
  • 1.25 Cup Water

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl until a dough forms.
  2. Knead the dough for 5 minutes.
  3. Roll out the dough and place it on a baking sheet.
  4. Add your favorite toppings.
  5. Bake at 450°F (232°C) for 15-20 minutes.
